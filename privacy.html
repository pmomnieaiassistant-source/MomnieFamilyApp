Momnie School Photos

Privacy policy. Last updated November 19, 2026.

Momnie School Photos is a small private tool that one family uses for itself. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Once a week it saves the school's weekly photos and updates into the family's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive, only files it creates. It creates folders and files in the account owner's own Drive (the photos, a short preview of each, and the week's text). It cannot see or change any other file in that Drive.
  • Gmail, read only, only one label. It reads the emails the account owner has filed under one Gmail label, only to find the link to that week's photo album. It does not send, change or delete email, and it does not read anything outside that label.
  • Public school web pages and shared album links that the family is allowed to view, to copy the week's text and photos.

What it does with the information

Everything is saved to the account owner's own Google Drive and is used only by the family. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train any model. Photos and text are not sent to any other service. The sign-in credentials are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private code repository.

Retention and deletion

Saved files stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can stop the tool and remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
